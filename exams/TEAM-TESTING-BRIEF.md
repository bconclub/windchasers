**What to try as an admin or instructor**

- The overview page, which shows completion rate, average score, accuracy, and
  which subjects the batch is losing marks on
- The question bank, with search and filters by subject, topic, difficulty and
  status
- Building an exam, either by picking questions yourself or by writing a
  blueprint such as thirty Navigation and twenty Meteorology and letting the
  system fill it
- The results page for the mock test, including the leaderboard and the per
  question analytics that show which questions people got wrong and which wrong
  option they picked

**What to try as a student**

- Sit DGCA Mock Test 1, which is twenty questions in thirty minutes with
  negative marking of 0.25 per wrong answer
- Use the question palette, mark a question for review, and submit
- Look at the result screen, the subject breakdown and the solution review
- Try practice mode, which is untimed with instant feedback

**Two things to know before you start**

Only one session per account is allowed. If two of you sign in with the same
login, the first person is signed out. Please take a different login each.

The questions currently loaded are placeholder content written to test the
system, not real DGCA material. The real question bank replaces them once the
Excel sheet is ready. All the student names and results are test data.

**Three things we already know about, no need to report them**

1. There is no Clear Response button in the test runner yet, so an answer
   cannot be returned to blank once selected
2. A total score never goes below zero, even when negative marking would take
   it there. We need your decision on whether that is correct for DGCA
3. The Add button on the students page needs a clearer label

**Reporting anything else**

Please send anything you find with the page you were on, what you expected, and
what happened instead. A screenshot helps.
